Which PayPal?

This is a real account. When you approve a reminder, PayPal emails a real customer. Oweleaf never sends without your approval, and reads each invoice again before sending.

WHERE TO FIND YOUR KEYS

Three steps in PayPal.

  1. Open the PayPal Developer Dashboard and go to Apps & Credentials.
  2. Choose Sandbox or Live at the top, then create an app or open an existing one.
  3. Copy the Client ID and Secret into this page. The app needs the Invoicing feature switched on.
  • Keys are checked against PayPal before anything is saved.
  • Stored with AES-256-GCM in this machine's data folder. The Secret is never shown again and never sent to the browser.
  • Only the owner can connect or disconnect. Five failed attempts pause connecting for fifteen minutes.
  • Switching account retires drafts written for the old one.

The encryption key is kept beside the data unless you set OWELEAF_VAULT_KEY. Protect this machine and its backups. Oweleaf is not affiliated with PayPal.
